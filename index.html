Historias que Pagan

Historias que Pagan is a small personal desktop tool used by the owner of the TikTok account "Historias que Pagan" to publish the short animated videos that the account creates.

Each video is reviewed and published manually: the owner sees a preview, chooses who can see it, accepts TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and presses Publish. Nothing is posted automatically.
