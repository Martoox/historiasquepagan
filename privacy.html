Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

What the app accesses

When the account owner connects TikTok, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (display name, avatar and the privacy options TikTok allows for the account). It uses them only to show who is posting and to publish the videos the owner confirms.

Where data is stored

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database, and it does not send data to anyone other than TikTok.

What the app does not do

It does not collect data from other TikTok users, does not sell or share any data, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not post anything without the owner's explicit confirmation.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time from TikTok's settings (Security > Manage app permissions) and delete the locally stored token.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the TikTok account "Historias que Pagan".
